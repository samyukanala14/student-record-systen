Merge Sort Recurrence Relation:

T(N) = 2T(N/2) + Θ(N) ⇒ By Master Theorem (Case 2): Θ(N log N)

• Divide Phase: Recursively partitions the student mark array into two halves at mid = left + (right - left)/2.

• Conquer Phase: Base cases of length 1 are trivially sorted.

• Merge Phase: Merges two sorted subarrays using ≥ comparison to ensure stability (students with identical marks retain their original relative order).
